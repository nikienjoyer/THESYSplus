3. Cite button on the thesis page

exists: Preview, Save, Downloadnew: Cite

Cite sits between Save and Download. Save and Cite both keep a reference for later, and Download stays last as the file action. It opens a small popover under the button that only formats and copies text. Copying never counts.

Phone: the bottom bar becomes [Save icon] [Cite icon] [Download] [Preview], so Cite is icon-only like Save.

What exists vs. what's new

  • Exists: ThesisCitation records (citing, cited, source, created_by, created_at), unique per pair, no self-citation. Duplicates can't count twice.
  • Exists: only approved citing theses count; the count shows in the card meta line; the thesis page lists citing title and year.
  • Exists: upload checklist (title match ticked, author+year unticked, manual add by search, save on press).
  • New: the Scholar-style link row and the quiet "Cited by 0"; authors, type and View on each cited-by row; the "+N you don't have access to" line.
  • New: a Cite button (none exists today) and the weak-match confirm card. The brief's "style" and "status" fields aren't on the model yet.
