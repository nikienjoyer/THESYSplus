Landing page: compact navbar

The whole landing page, drawn at a real screen width and shrunk to fit. Pick a screen, then flip between today's navbar and the compact one. Only the navbar changes; everything else is today's page.

Compact: the links stop stretching with the screen. They sit as one centered group with an even 2rem gap (2.5rem from 1536px), so the space between "Home" and "Analytics" stays about the width of the hero headline instead of most of the bar. Link text, padding, the underline on the current page and the hover pill are unchanged.

Fluid: the gap between tabs is 2.5% of the screen width (never under 1rem, never over 5rem): 32px on a 1280 laptop, 38px at 1536, 48px on a 24" monitor, 64px at 2560. Bigger screens get more air, smaller ones stay tight, and no screen gets the stretched look. Code: NAV_LIST_CLASS becomes xl:gap-[clamp(1rem,2.5vw,5rem)], dropping the vw widths and justify-between.

Code it would take (compact): one constant in AppNavbar.jsx: NAV_LIST_CLASS drops xl:w-[52vw] 2xl:w-[58vw] xl:justify-between for xl:gap-8 2xl:gap-10. Every app page and the landing page share it.
