Rules behind the screen

  • Detected against approved and pending theses. Rejected theses are never cited.
  • Saved at upload, all of them. Nothing has to run again when a cited thesis is approved later.
  • "Cited by N" counts a citation only when both the citing and the cited thesis are approved.
  • Under review tag on any cited thesis that is still pending.
  • Privacy: another student's pending title is never shown, only "1 more thesis that is still under review" (or "N more theses…"). Faculty and admins, who can already see pending theses, get the titles.
  • Unchanged: when everything is already approved, the screen stays as today ("Each one now counts this thesis under "Cited by"").
